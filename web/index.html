What the marks mean

Estimates, not guarantees: only each file's header is read (about 6 MB per quant, never the weights), the KV cache is sized as f16, and the placement is the app's own stand-in for llama-fit-params. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to us. Source: LlamaToaster · extension.
